How to read results

Two separate questions

FODMAP is about fermentable carbohydrates and portion size. A food can be gluten-free and still high FODMAP.

Gluten-free is about wheat, barley, rye and cross-contact. A food can be low FODMAP and still contain gluten.

Depends means the recipe, brand, portion, or preparation changes the answer.

Useful label habits

  • • Search for onion, garlic, inulin/chicory, honey and concentrated fruit juices.
  • • For coeliac disease, look for certified gluten-free controls—not only “no wheat”.
  • • Check serving size: FODMAP results can change at larger portions.
  • • Restaurant dishes need a recipe check; sauces and stocks are common hidden sources.

Health note

This app is an educational screening tool, not a diagnosis or a substitute for a registered dietitian. If you have coeliac disease, an allergy, or significant symptoms, use medical guidance and the manufacturer’s current label.
